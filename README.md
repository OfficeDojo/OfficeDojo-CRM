# OfficeDojo

OfficeDojo is a real estate CRM platform for agents and small teams. It brings client and contact management, property search, transaction tracking, document collaboration, and team workflows together in one workspace.

- **Website:** [officedojo.com](https://officedojo.com)
- **Client portal:** [app.officedojo.com](https://app.officedojo.com)
- **Dojo Connect Portal:** [officedojo.com/dojo-connect/login](https://www.officedojo.com/dojo-connect/login)

## What OfficeDojo offers

### CRM and client management

- **Dashboard:** View business activity and key sales metrics.
- **Contacts:** Organize clients and leads, manage contact details, add notes and reminders, and connect contacts to transactions.
- **Agent profile:** Maintain an agent profile and public-facing information.
- **Activity history:** Review recent CRM activity.

### Property search

- Search property listings by location and other listing details.
- View listings in grid or map layouts.
- Review property details, listing information, and MLS identifiers when available.
- Share public property listings where supported.

### Sales Tracker and transactions

- Track real estate transactions and their progress.
- Manage transaction milestones, contacts, financial details, and status.
- Organize transaction documents, request reviews, and follow document status.
- View sales reports and transaction history.

### Dojo Connect

- Invite managers and other collaborators to help with Sales Tracker work.
- Provide invited managers access through a dedicated helper portal.
- Collaborate on transaction documents and notes, with updates visible to the CRM agent.
- Manage invitations and collaborator access.

### Communication and productivity

- **Messages and chat:** Communicate with clients and collaborators in the context of a transaction or conversation.
- **Calendar and reminders:** Keep track of appointments, milestones, and follow-ups.
- **Tasks:** Coordinate work where enabled for the account.
- **Dojo Sign:** Support document signing workflows.
- **Dojo AI:** AI-assisted tools within the CRM.

### Administration and subscription management

- Manage subscribers, account plans, billing, and subscription status.
- Manage team access, roles, and sales representatives.
- Configure website visibility and business settings.
- Compose newsletters and manage subscriber communications.
- Review business, subscription, and sales activity.

## Who it is for

OfficeDojo is built for real estate professionals who want to manage client relationships, property and transaction workflows, and team collaboration in one place.

Visit [officedojo.com](https://officedojo.com) to learn about OfficeDojo or schedule a demo. Existing users can sign in through the [Client Portal](https://app.officedojo.com).
